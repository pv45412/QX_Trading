# Research Specification

## Purpose

Research owns depth and complexity that would distract from a live trade decision. It
supports symbol, ETF, sector, historical, strategy, and long-term thesis investigation
without publishing a competing canonical lifecycle.

## Research Modes

### Instrument

- symbol overview and provenance
- historical structure and regime context
- relative strength and benchmark comparison
- supported fundamental/qualitative context
- links to current canonical evaluation in Trade Workspace

### ETF / Sector

- sector rotation and relative-strength rankings
- ETF constituents and fund-weight concentration
- constituent contribution and freshness
- market/sector comparison

The current `Holdings` component belongs here under an explicit ETF/fund label.

### Options and Strategy

- exploratory options ideas and payoff modeling
- strategy legs, assumptions, and max loss/profit
- distinction between modeled research output and canonical options expression
- no suggestion that a modeled idea passed tradeability or portfolio risk

### Historical Structure

- BOS/CHoCH and swing history when supported
- multi-timeframe comparison with explicit timeframe labels
- data provenance and point-in-time timestamp
- no hindsight data in historical/replay views

### Backtesting / Validation

- strategy research and canonical replay summaries
- state transitions, signal R, and tradeability R clearly distinguished
- MAE/MFE and segmentation when generated by approved replay tooling
- no automatic rule or threshold optimization

### Long-Term Thesis

- thesis statement and evidence when a durable contract exists
- sector/relative-strength context
- historical structure
- linked broker-position risk from Portfolio
- review cadence and provenance

## Navigation Within Research

Proposed secondary modes:

```text
Instrument | ETF & Sector | Options & Strategy | Historical | Backtests
```

These are modes of one destination. A route should only remain distinct when its data,
controls, and result are genuinely distinct.

## Current Component Placement

- `QXResearchStudio`: refactor into the Instrument mode; remove static/generated claims
  or label them clearly.
- `ReferenceRanking` and `RSLine`: reuse in ETF & Sector / Instrument.
- `Holdings`: reuse after renaming to ETF Constituents; never call it Portfolio.
- `QXOptionsIdeas`: retain as modeled research with visible provenance.
- `QXStrategyWorkspaces`: retain as strategy/payoff research after removing hard-coded
  endpoint assumptions.
- `AIReview`: legacy-only until its evidence and canonical relationship are explicit;
  it cannot replace canonical WHY.
- `SPXFuturesWorkspace`: split deeper historical/context panels into Research while
  keeping compact market context on Today.

## Handoff to Trade Workspace

Research may send:

- symbol
- selected horizon/timeframe
- research record or snapshot identifier
- user-selected thesis context

Trade Workspace independently requests the canonical V2 evaluation. Research cannot
send a client-computed lifecycle state or override canonical evidence.

## Provenance Requirements

Every research output should identify one of:

- canonical pipeline result
- source-backed market data
- modeled calculation
- static editorial content
- user-entered thesis
- legacy analysis

Mixed panels must label each section. “AI” or “institutional” branding is not
provenance.

## Non-Goals

- no live order workflow
- no portfolio approval calculation
- no client-side market-driver inference
- no automatic strategy optimization
- no relabeling of exploratory options as canonical options suitability
